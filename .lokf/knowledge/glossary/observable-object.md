# ObservableObject

In the Unified Cyber Ontology, an `ObservableObject` is a distinct article or unit within the digital domain: a file, a device, an account, a process or a network connection, among many. The schema renders it as `ObservableObject`, an `Observable` with `Item` mixed in, and 95 classes sit directly under it.[^uco_observable.yaml]
